**Hermes Agent** is Nous Research's open-source agent, released in February 2026 with the tagline "the agent that grows with you" and a focus on multi-level memory and a gateway that puts every client behind one endpoint ([MarkTechPost](https://www.marktechpost.com/2026/02/26/nous-research-releases-hermes-agent-to-fix-ai-forgetfulness-with-multi-level-memory-and-dedicated-remote-terminal-access-support/), [AlphaSignal](https://alphasignal.ai/news/nous-research-s-hermes-agent-unifies-every-client-behind-one-gateway)). By late 2026 its repository had reportedly passed 100,000 GitHub stars ([release tracker](https://petronellatech.com/blog/hermes-agent-ai-guide-2026/)). <Evidence level="secondary" />

## Persistent Goals (`/goal`)

The Hermes docs are unusually direct about lineage: `/goal` is "our take on the Ralph loop" ([docs](https://hermes-agent.nousresearch.com/docs/user-guide/features/goals), [source](https://github.com/nousresearch/hermes-agent/blob/main/website/docs/user-guide/features/goals.md)).

## Goals × kanban × cron

Hermes draws a clear line between one goal and many:

- **One task that should keep iterating** → `/goal`, or a kanban card created with `--goal`.
- **Many independent tasks with dependencies, handoffs or several profiles** → **Kanban**. Each card can be its own goal loop; the board is the orchestrator.
- **Recurring or conditional work** → **cron**. Community posts describe combining cron with goals for "personal AI operating system" setups ([YanXbt](https://yanxbt.substack.com/p/hermes-agent-as-a-personal-ai-operating), [Hermes Bible](https://www.hermesbible.com/flows/hermes-as-personal-ai-operating-system)).

A viral post claimed one `/goal` produced "100 landing pages, a map of clinics, and 100 drafted emails. … Zero babysitting." ([X](https://x.com/IBuzovskyi/status/2067479409645257075)) <Evidence level="anecdotal" />

## Cost controls

The cheap-judge routing is a cost lever in its own right. Release coverage says v0.23 added **hard spending limits at the gateway layer**, one of the few dollar-denominated budgets among the major tools ([release tracker](https://petronellatech.com/blog/hermes-agent-ai-guide-2026/)). <Evidence level="secondary" />
