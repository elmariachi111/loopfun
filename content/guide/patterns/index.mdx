---
title: Loop patterns
description: The recurring shapes that agentic loops take. Each is a different answer to "what triggers the next iteration, and what ends it?"
icon: Boxes
---

Every loop answers two questions: **what triggers the next iteration** and **what ends it**. The patterns below are the common answers. They mix freely: a Ralph loop can contain a maker/checker step, a heartbeat can start a goal loop, and a graph is mostly loops wired together.

| Pattern | Next iteration triggered by | Ends when | Typical use |
|---|---|---|---|
| [Ralph loop](/guide/patterns/ralph) | The previous iteration ending | Completion promise, iteration cap, or never | Grinding through a backlog or spec |
| [Maker/checker](/guide/patterns/maker-checker) | The checker rejecting the work | The checker approves | Anything where quality matters |
| [Metric loop](/guide/patterns/metric-loops) | A finished experiment | Budget exhausted; best result kept | Optimisation with a clear number |
| [Ambient loop](/guide/patterns/ambient-loops) | A timer or an event | Never; it's a standing process | Babysitting, monitoring, inboxes |
| [Graphs and teams](/guide/patterns/graphs-and-teams) | An orchestrator, or another node | The orchestrator decides | Large or parallel projects |
